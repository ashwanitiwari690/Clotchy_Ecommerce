OUR STORY

Built for people who don't blend in.

Clotchcy started as a small idea: apparel shouldn't just be worn, it should say something. What began as a handful of graphic tees for friends has grown into a full streetwear label serving thousands of customers who wear their identity with pride — from spiritual seekers to pop-culture fans to everyday warriors.

Every design starts with a story, every fabric is chosen for comfort as much as style, and every order is packed with the same care as the first one we ever shipped.
